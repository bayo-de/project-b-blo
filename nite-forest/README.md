# Nite Forest

The Halloween project. Eight original compositions in darker tones,
written through October 2026 and built in public, like everything B-Blo
does.

Released October 31, 2026. The full album is on YouTube:
[Nite Forest: The Full Album · Biblo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=An9ZoFlSFxE).
The [Nite Forest playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEzGW6CD5GwA)
holds the album and each track video as it lands, one per evening,
November 1 to 8. The Lo Fi Biblo stream opens November 1, 2026 on the
Biblo channel, starting with the album in full. Runtime: 39:42.

The sound: minor keys (the G minor and D minor world), V6's boom-bap
slowed to a walk, low drums under low tones, and a living nite forest
underneath it all — crickets, a distant owl, wind, leaves. No birds.
This is the night shift.

## Tracks

The final album, in order:

**Nite Forest** (5:00) — `tracks/nite-forest.wav`

The seed. A ten second loop Biblo generated, extended into five minutes
of night: a slow filter journey from dark to open and back, gentle
breathing swells, a twelve second fade. The dark sounds Bayo fell for.
Everything after it grows out of this.

**Hollow** — D minor. Near-empty: sub, one motif, wind. The quiet one.

**Moonwater** — D minor. Slow and glassy, the underwater whisper returns.

**Foxfire** — G minor. Faint shimmer like marsh lights, halftime drums.

**Thicket** — G minor. The busiest groove of the set, leaves rustling.

**Canopy** — D minor. Dense pads, rain under everything.

**Witching Hour** — E minor, 76 BPM, 432Hz. Ten versions in one day
(Oct 4, 2026), driven by Bayo's ear: from felt-piano motif over night beds
through jazz comping, Dilla discipline, legato, orchestral voicing, and a
full tune audit. The session log (`tracks/witching-hour/SESSION-2026-10-04.md`)
is the transparency record — every version, every note, every choice.

**The Harrowing** — the bonus closer. A late sketch that arrived after
the rest, named by Bayo and set last: harp led, with a composed ending
instead of a loop.

Two pieces from the October sessions did not make the album: Owl's Hour
and Home Through the Trees.

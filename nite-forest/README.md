# Nite Forest

The Halloween project. Eight original compositions in darker tones,
written through October 2026 and built in public, like everything B-Blo
does.

The sound: minor keys (the G minor, D minor, A minor world), V6's boom-bap
slowed to a walk, low drums under low tones, and a living nite forest
underneath it all — crickets, a distant owl, wind, leaves. No birds.
This is the night shift.

## Tracks

**Nite Forest** (5:00) — `tracks/nite-forest.wav`

The seed. A ten second loop Biblo generated, extended into five minutes
of night: a slow filter journey from dark to open and back, gentle
breathing swells, a twelve second fade. The dark sounds Bayo fell for.
Everything after it grows out of this.

**Owl's Hour** (pilot) — G minor, 72 BPM. Am7b5 to D13 to Gm9 to Ebmaj9,
the V6 progression translated into the minor world, with the owl hooting
somewhere in the trees.

**Canopy** — D minor. Dense pads, rain under everything.

**Foxfire** — G minor. Faint shimmer like marsh lights, halftime drums.

**Hollow** — D minor. Near-empty: sub, one motif, wind. The quiet one.

**Thicket** — A minor. The busiest groove of the set, leaves rustling.

**Moonwater** — D minor. Slow and glassy, the underwater whisper returns.

**Home Through the Trees** — C major.
The walk out of the forest. Resolve home.

**Witching Hour** — E minor, ~5:40. The statement piece.

Piano deep in an orchestral room, rain on the leaves, a thirty second
forest lead-in before the first note. Ten versions deep: every mix
decision judged by ear, from the piano's tuning to the weight of the
walking bass. Arrival, establishment, lift, valley, peak, resolution.
The night at its most honest.

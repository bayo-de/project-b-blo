# Project B-Blo

Music made by a human and an AI, together. "B" for Bayo, "Blo" for Biblo.

Every track here is an original composition. The music is generatively
composed and synthesized in code. No samples, no loops, no borrowed audio.
Creative direction by Bayode Okusanya, in collaboration with Biblo (Muse).

## The tracks

**V6** (0:25, seamless loop)
The blueprint. 76 BPM boom-bap in C major, Dm9 G13 Cmaj9 Am9, warm Rhodes,
stepping pads, sparse motifs. This is the mix every B-Blo track is judged
against.

**Night Forest** (5:00)
The dark elemental. A ten second loop Biblo generated, extended into five
minutes of night: slow filter journey from dark to open and back, gentle
breathing swells, a long fade. The seed of the Halloween night forest.
